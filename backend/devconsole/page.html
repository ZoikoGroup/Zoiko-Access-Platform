Zoiko Access Dev Console

DEV ONLY - no login, never expose

1. Tenant

A tenant is one customer inside this cell. Everything below happens inside the selected tenant, and no other tenant can see it.

2. Organization

A clinic, lab or company inside the tenant. Roles are held at an organization.

3. Person (actor)

Someone who can log in. Registering the same identity twice returns the same person.

4. Give a role

A role says what someone may do at an organization. By itself it grants nothing; the access check in step 5 decides.

5. Patients and who may reach them

A patient is a record, separate from a login. A clinician needs a care relationship, a guardian needs an approved proxy, and a patient reaches their own record through their login. Without a link, step 6 refuses patient data.

6. Access check - may this person do this?

Asks the real policy engine. Default is deny. Every check is recorded and audited, allowed or not.
